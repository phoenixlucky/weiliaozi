# 兵教上 · Military Training, Part I

> **终极整合版**：以武经七书本为底本。本章仅存武经七书本一种版本，无其他版本异文可资参校，整合版即其全文。
>
> *Ultimate reconstructed text: based on the Wujing Qishu recension. This chapter survives only in the Wujing Qishu recension, with no variant readings from other versions available for collation; the reconstructed text is therefore its complete text.*

## 终极整合版 · Ultimate Reconstructed Text

### 1

**兵教上:** 兵之教，令分营居陈，有非令而进退者，加犯教之罪。前行者前行教之，后行者后行教之，左行者左行教之，右行者右行教之，教举五人，其甲首有赏。弗教如犯教之罪。罗地者，自揭其伍，伍内互揭之，免其罪。

The instruction regarding military training states that soldiers should be ordered to divide their camps and take positions; those who advance or retreat without following orders shall be punished for violating the regulations. Those who proceed forward should be trained in advancing, those who move backward should be trained in retreating, those moving to the left should be trained in leftward movement, and those moving to the right should be trained in rightward movement. When five people are properly instructed, their officer shall receive a reward. Those who do not follow the training shall be punished as if they have violated the regulations. A person who commits a land-related offense should report himself within his group of five; if members within the same group report each other, their punishment shall be waived.

### 2

**兵教上:** 凡伍临陈，若一人有不进死于敌，则教者如犯法之罪。凡什保什，若亡一人，而九人不尽死于敌，则教者如犯教之罪。自什己上，至于裨将，有不若法者，则教者如犯法者之罪。

Whenever a group is in battle formation and one person fails to advance and dies at the hands of the enemy, the instructor shall be punished as if he has violated the law. Whenever a unit of ten is responsible for guarding each other, and one person goes missing while the remaining nine do not all die fighting the enemy, then the instructor shall be punished according to the regulations for violating military discipline. From units of ten upward, up to the level of assistant generals, if anyone fails to comply with the regulations, then their instructors shall be punished as those who have violated the law.

### 3

**兵教上:** 凡明刑罚，正劝赏，必在乎兵教之法。

To clarify punishments and establish proper rewards, it is essential to rely on the laws governing military training.

### 4

**兵教上:** 将异其旗，卒异其章，左军章左肩，右军章右肩，中军章胸前。书其章曰：某甲、某士。前后军各五行，尊章置首上，其次差降之。

The general should use a different flag, and soldiers should wear distinct insignias: the left army's insignia on the left shoulder, the right army's insignia on the right shoulder, and the center army's insignia in front of the chest. The insignias should be marked with: \"a certain officer\" and \"a certain soldier.\" The front and rear armies each have five ranks; the highest-ranking insignias are placed on the head, with those of lower rank arranged in descending order.

### 5

**兵教上:** 伍长教其四人，以板为鼓，以瓦为金，以竿为旗。击鼓而进，低旗则趋，击金而退。麾而左之，麾而右之，金鼓俱击而坐。

The leader of a group of five should train his four subordinates, using wooden boards as drums, clay tiles as bells, and poles as flags. Beat the drum to advance, lower the flag to quicken pace, and strike the bell to retreat. Wave the banner to the left or right for movement in that direction; when both the bell and drum are struck, sit down.

### 6

**兵教上:** 伍长教成，合之什长。什长教成，合之卒长。卒长教成，合之伯长。伯长教成，合之兵尉。兵尉教成，合之裨将。裨将教成，合之大将。大将教之，陈于中野，置大表三百步而一。既阵去表，百步而决。百步而趋，百步而鹜，习战以成其节，乃为之赏罚。

Once a group leader's training is complete, he should be combined with the leader of another group to form a unit of ten. When the leader of a unit of ten completes his training, he should be grouped together with other units under the command of a squad leader. Once the squad leader's training is completed, he should be combined into a unit led by a captain. When the captain completes his training, his unit should be incorporated under the command of an officer in charge of military affairs. Once the officer in charge of military affairs has completed their training, they should be combined into units led by assistant generals. When an assistant general completes his training, he and his forces are incorporated under the command of the supreme general. The supreme general oversees this training, arranging troops in an open field and setting up a large marker every three hundred paces. Once the formation is established, remove the markers at intervals of one hundred paces to determine positions clearly. Advance quickly for a hundred paces, then slow down for another hundred paces; through repeated practice of battle formations and discipline, rewards and punishments are then applied accordingly.

### 7

**兵教上:** 自尉吏而下，尽有旗。战胜得旗者，各视所得之爵，以明赏劝之心。

From the rank of officer in charge downward, all ranks must have flags. Those who capture enemy flags during a victorious battle shall be rewarded according to the rank of the flag they captured, in order to clearly demonstrate the intent for rewards and encouragement.

### 8

**兵教上:** 战胜在乎立威，立威在乎戮力，戮力在乎正罚，正罚者所以明赏也。

Victory in battle depends on establishing authority; establishing authority depends on uniting strength; uniting strength depends on enforcing proper punishments. Proper punishment is what makes rewards clear and effective.

### 9

**兵教上:** 令民背国门之限，决生死之分，教之死而不疑者，有以也。令守者必固，战者必斗，奸谋不作，奸民不语，令行无变，兵行无猜，轻者若霆，奋敌若惊。举功别德，明如白黑，令民从上令，如四肢应心也。

Order the people to leave behind the limits of their homeland, face the division between life and death, and train them to be willing to die without hesitation—there is a reason for this. Orders must ensure that defenders are resolute, warriors fight bravely, treacherous plots do not arise, and disloyal people remain silent. Orders must be carried out without deviation, and military actions must proceed without suspicion; swift as thunder in movement, fierce enough to startle the enemy into alarm. Recognize achievements and distinguish virtue clearly, as distinctly as black from white; command the people to obey orders from above as naturally as a body's limbs respond to the mind.

### 10

**兵教上:** 前军绝行乱陈，破坚如溃者，有以也。此谓之兵教。所以开封疆，守社稷，除患害，成武德也。

The vanguard that cuts through enemy lines and disrupts their formation, breaking strong defenses as if they were collapsing—this is done for a reason. This is what is called military training. This is how one expands territory, defends the state, removes dangers and threats, and establishes martial virtue.
