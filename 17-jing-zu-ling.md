# 经卒令 · Regulations on Troop Organization

## 1

**经卒令:** 经卒者，以经令分之分为三分焉：左军苍旗，卒戴苍羽；右军白旗，卒戴白羽；中军黄旗，卒戴黄羽。卒有五章：前一行苍章，次二行赤章，次三行黄章，次四行白章，次五行黑章。

The soldiers of the Jing formation were divided into three parts by the Jing Ling: the Left Army with dark blue banners, and the soldiers wore dark blue feathers on their helmets. The Right Army had white banners, and the soldiers wore white feathers on their helmets; the Central Army had yellow banners, and the soldiers wore yellow feathers on their helmets. The soldiers had five patterns: the first row in front bore dark blue insignias, the next two rows red insignias, followed by three rows with yellow insignias, then four rows with white insignias, and finally five rows with black insignias.

## 2

**经卒令:** 次以经卒，亡章者有诛，前一五行，置章于首；次二五行，置章于项；次三五行，置章于胸；次四五行，置章于腹；次五五行，置章于腰。如此，卒无非其吏，吏无非其卒，见非而不诘，见乱而不禁，其罪如之。

Next came the Jing soldiers; those without insignias were punished. For the first five ranks, place the insignia on their heads; for the next ten ranks, place the insignias around their necks; for the following fifteen ranks, place the insignias on their chests; for the subsequent twenty ranks, place the insignias over their bellies; and for the final twenty-five ranks, place the insignias at their waists. In this way, soldiers would not be under the wrong officers, and officers would not command the wrong soldiers. If one sees something wrong but does not question it, or witnesses disorder but does not stop it, their punishment shall be as such.

## 3

**经卒令:** 鼓行交斗，则前行进为犯难，后行进为辱众。逾五行而前进者有赏，逾五行而后者有诛，所以知进退先后，吏卒之功也。故曰：「鼓之前如霆，动如风雨，莫敢当其前，莫敢蹑其后。」言有经也。

When drums sound and the formations engage in battle, advancing while at the front ranks is considered braving danger, whereas advancing from the rear ranks is regarded as dishonoring the troops. Those who advance beyond five ranks will be rewarded, while those who fall back beyond five ranks will be punished. This is how the officers and soldiers distinguish between advancing and retreating, and it reflects their competence. Therefore it is said: \"The advance before the drum sounds is as thunder, and movement is like wind and rain. None dares to oppose its front, nor follow from behind.\" This refers to the established formations.
