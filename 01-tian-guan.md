# 天官 · Heavenly Officials

中文原文和英文翻译对照，每段先中文后英文。

## 1

**天官:** 梁惠王问尉缭子曰："黄帝刑德，可以百胜，有之乎？"尉缭子对曰："刑以伐之，德以守之，非所谓天官时日阴阳向背也。黄帝者，人事而已矣。

Liang Huiwang asked Wei Liaozi, "Is it true that the Yellow Emperor's penal and moral virtues could ensure a hundred victories?" Wei Liaozi replied, "Penal measures are used to attack, and moral virtues are used to defend; this is not what is meant by the heavenly officials, auspicious days, yin-yang, or directional advantages. The Yellow Emperor was merely a man who attended to human affairs."

## 2

**天官:** "今有城，东西攻不能取；南北攻不能取；四方岂无顺时乘之者耶？然不能取者，城高池深，兵器备具，财谷多积，豪士一谋者也。若城下池浅守弱，则取之矣。由此观之，天官时日不若人事也。

"Now there is a city; attacking it from the east and west cannot capture it; attacking it from the north and south cannot capture it. Surely there are not people in all four directions who do not take advantage of favorable times to attack? Yet they cannot capture it because the city walls are high, the moats deep, weapons and equipment fully prepared, grain and wealth abundantly stored, and there is one capable man who devises strategies. If the city walls are low, the moats shallow, and the defenders weak, then it can be captured. From this we see that heavenly officials and auspicious days are not as important as human affairs."

## 3

**天官:** "按天官曰：'背水阵为绝地，向阪阵为废军。'武王伐纣，背济水向山阪而阵，以二万二千五百人，击纣之亿万而灭商，岂纣不得天官之阵哉！

"It is said in the Heavenly Officials' doctrine: 'To set up an army with its back to water is a place of certain death; to face a slope for battle is to bring about the ruin of one's troops.' When King Wu attacked Zhou, he set up his army with its back to the Ji River and facing a mountain slope. With 22,500 men, he defeated Zhou's millions and destroyed the Shang dynasty. Was it because Zhou failed to employ the heavenly officials' formations?"

## 4

**天官:** "楚将公子心与齐人战，时有彗星出，柄在齐。柄所在胜，不可击。公子心曰：'彗星何知？以彗斗者固倒而胜焉。'明日与齐战，大破之。

"A Chu general, Gongzi Xin, fought against the Qi people. At that time, a comet appeared with its handle pointing toward Qi. The side where the comet's handle points will be victorious; it cannot be attacked. Gongzi Xin said, 'What does a comet know? He who fights relying on the comet will inevitably be overturned and defeated.' The next day, he fought against Qi and utterly crushed them."

## 5

**天官:** "黄帝曰：'先神先鬼，先稽我智。'谓之天官人事而已。"

"The Yellow Emperor said: 'First consult the gods and ghosts, but first examine my own wisdom.' It is merely a matter of heavenly officials and human affairs."

## 📖 群书治要异文

《群书治要》卷三十七收录的《天官》篇为唐代抄本选段，与武经七书本有重要文字差异。以下为群书治要版全文：

### 1

**天官:** 梁惠王问尉缭子曰：吾闻黄帝有刑德，可以百战百胜，其有之乎。尉缭曰：不然，黄帝所谓刑德者，以刑伐之，以德守之，非世之所谓刑德也。世之所谓刑德者，天官时日、阴阳向背者也。黄帝者人事而已矣。何以言之，今有城于此，从其东西攻之，不能取，从其南北攻之不能取，此四者，岂不得顺时乘利者哉。然不能取者何，城高池深，兵战（本书战作器）备具，谋而守之也。若乃城下池浅守弱，可取也。由（旧作犹，改之）是观之，天官时日，不若人事也。

*Liang Huiwang asked Wei Liaozi, "I have heard that the Yellow Emperor had Xingde, which enabled him to win a hundred battles out of a hundred. Is there such a thing?" Wei Liaozi said, "Not quite. What the Yellow Emperor called Xingde meant using punishment to attack and virtue to defend; it was not what people in later generations referred to as Xingde." What people in later generations called Xingde refers to the heavenly officials, auspicious days and hours, and considerations of yin-yang orientation. The Yellow Emperor was concerned only with human affairs. Why do I say this? Suppose there is a city here; if we attack it from the east or west, we cannot capture it. If we attack it from the north or south, we still cannot capture it. In these four directions, could it be that we are not following an auspicious time or taking advantage of favorable conditions? Yet if it cannot be captured, why is that? It is because the city walls are high and the moats deep, the military equipment (the original text has qi for zhan here) is fully prepared, and there is strategy in its defense. If, however, the city walls are low and the moats shallow, and the defenders are weak, then it can be captured. From this (the original text previously had you for yóu, which was corrected) we can see that heavenly officials and auspicious days are not as important as human affairs.*

### 2

**天官:** 故按刑德天官之陈曰：背水陈者为绝地，向坂陈者为废军，武王之伐纣也。背济水，向山之阪，以万二千人，击纣之亿有八万人，断纣头悬之白旗，纣岂不得天官之陈哉。然不得胜者何，人事不得也。黄帝曰：先稽己智者，谓之天官，以是观之，人事而已矣。

*Therefore, according to the arrangements of Xingde and heavenly officials: forming a formation with one's back to water is called an isolated position; forming a formation facing a slope is considered a doomed army. When King Wu attacked Zhou, he had his back to the Ji River and faced a mountain slope, with 12,000 men attacking Zhou's forces of 80 million. He cut off Zhou's head and displayed it on a white flag. Was Zhou not following the heavenly official formations? Yet why did he fail to win? Because human affairs were not properly managed. The Yellow Emperor said, "First examining one's own wisdom is called the heavenly official." From this we can see, it is only about human affairs.*

> 主要异文对照：武经七书本作"二万二千五百人"击"亿万"，群书治要本作"万二千人"击"亿有八万人"；武经七书本有公子心破彗星事，群书治要本无。

## 📖 类书引文

宋代《太平御览·天部七·妖星》引《尉缭子》佚文，与武经七书本公子心破彗星事同源，但人名作"将军子正"而非"公子心"，文辞亦异，可资校勘：

**太平御览引《尉缭子》曰：** 昔楚将军子正与齐战，未合，初夜，彗星出，柄在齐，所在胜，不可击。子正曰："彗何知？"明日与齐战，大破之。

*The Taiping Yulan quotes the Wei Liaozi: In ancient times, the Chu general Zizheng fought against Qi. Before the armies engaged, at the beginning of the night, a comet appeared with its handle pointing toward Qi. Those who say that wherever the handle points will be victorious, and it cannot be attacked. Zizheng said: "What does a comet know?" The next day, he fought against Qi and utterly crushed them.*

> 异文分析：「公子心」与「将军子正」当为同一人，盖「子正」为名，「公子」为身份称谓。武经七书本多"以彗斗者固倒而胜焉"一句，太平御览引文无此句而有"初夜"二字，详略互见。

清代孙诒让《墨子闲诂》引《尉缭子》载同一事的又一异文，人名作"将军子心"：

**墨子闲诂引《尉缭子》曰：** 昔楚将军子心与齐人战，未合，夜彗星出，柄在齐，所在胜，不可击。子心曰："彗星何知！"明日与齐人战大破之。

*Sun Yirang's Mozi Jiangu quotes the Wei Liaozi: In ancient times, the Chu general Zixin fought against Qi. Before the armies engaged, at night a comet appeared with its handle pointing toward Qi. It was said that wherever the handle points will be victorious, and it cannot be attacked. Zixin said: "What does a comet know!" The next day, he fought against the Qi people and utterly crushed them.*

> 异文演变：「公子心」→「将军子正」→「将军子心」。三处人名各不相同，当为传抄讹变。又「初夜」太平御览妖星条作「初夜」，墨子闲诂引作「夜」，武经七书本无时间状语。

同书《兵部三十二·阵》又引《尉缭子》异文，将梁惠王问刑德与武王伐纣合为一则：

**太平御览·兵部三十二引《尉缭子》曰：** 梁惠王问尉缭曰："吾闻黄帝有刑德，可以百战百胜，有之乎！"尉缭曰："不然。黄帝所谓刑德者，天官时日、阴阳背向者也。黄帝者，人事而已矣。何以言之？武王伐纣，背清水，向山陵，以万二千击纣之亿有八万，断纣头悬于白旗，岂不得天官之阵哉！"

*The Taiping Yulan (Military Affairs 32: Formations) quotes the Wei Liaozi: King Hui of Liang asked Wei Liao, "I have heard that the Yellow Emperor had Xingde, which could ensure a hundred victories in a hundred battles. Is this so?" Wei Liao said, "Not at all. What the Yellow Emperor called Xingde refers to heavenly officials, auspicious days, and considerations of yin-yang orientation. The Yellow Emperor was merely a man who attended to human affairs. Why do I say this? When King Wu attacked Zhou, he had his back to the Qingshui River, facing a mountain ridge, with 12,000 men attacking Zhou's 80 million. He cut off Zhou's head and hung it on a white flag. Was this not a formation of the heavenly officials?"*

> 异文分析：本条与武经七书本相比，有几点重要差异：(1) 直接以"天官时日、阴阳背向"定义刑德，省去了"刑以伐之，德以守之"的正面阐释；(2) "背清水""向山陵"与武经七书本"背济水""向山阪"不同——"清水"当为"济水"之误，因济水古称"清水"之一段；(3) 与群书治要本一致作"万二千"击"亿有八万"；(4) 全段省略了"今有城于此"的攻城比喻，直接引出武王伐纣例证，可见唐代所见《尉缭子》文本已存在简繁差异。
