# 攻权 · Strategic Strength in Attack

## 1

**攻权:** 兵以静胜，国以专胜。

Victory in war is achieved through stillness; victory in the state is achieved through unity.

## 2

**攻权:** 力分者弱，心疑者背。夫力弱故进退不豪，纵敌不擒，将吏士卒动静一身。心既疑背，则计决而不动，动决而不禁，异口虚言。将无修容，卒无常试，发攻必衄，是谓疾陵之兵，无足与斗。

When strength is divided, it becomes weak; when the heart wavers, it turns away. Because their strength is weak, they are not bold in advancing or retreating; even when the enemy is within reach, they do not capture them. The movements and stillness of officers, soldiers, and common troops all depend on one person. When the heart is doubtful and turns away, then plans are made but not carried out, actions are decided upon but not restrained, and conflicting false words emerge. If a general lacks discipline and soldiers are never tested regularly, then when an attack is launched, it will surely suffer defeat. This is called a hasty and reckless army, unworthy of battle.

## 3

**攻权:** 将帅者心也，群下者支节也。其心动以诚，则支节必力；其心动以疑，则支节必背。夫将不心制，卒不节动，虽胜幸胜也，非攻权也。

A general is the heart; the subordinates are the limbs and joints. If the heart acts with sincerity, then the limbs and joints will surely exert strength; If the heart is filled with doubt, then the limbs and joints must turn away. When a general does not control his mind, and soldiers do not act in coordination, even if they win, it is merely a stroke of luck, not the result of strategic strength.

## 4

**攻权:** 夫民无两畏也，畏我侮敌，畏敌侮我。见侮者败，立威者胜。凡将能其道者，吏畏其将也；吏畏其将者，民畏其吏也；民畏其吏者，敌畏其民也。是故，知胜败之道者，必先知畏侮之权。

The people have no dual fears; they fear either being humiliated by the enemy or humiliating the enemy. Those who are insulted will be defeated; those who establish authority will win. A general who can follow the right path will have his subordinates fear him; When subordinates fear their general, the people will fear their officials; When the people fear their officials, the enemy will fear its people. Therefore, those who understand the way of victory and defeat must first understand the power dynamics between being feared and being insulted.

## 5

**攻权:** 夫不爱悦其心者，不我用也；不严畏其心者，不我举也。爱在下顺，威在上立，爱故不二，威故不犯。故善将者，爱与威而已。

He who does not cherish and please my heart will not be used by me; He who is not strict and instills fear in the hearts of others will not be promoted by me. Love lies in subordinates' obedience, authority lies in a superior's establishment; love ensures loyalty without division, and authority prevents transgression. Therefore, an excellent general needs nothing more than love and authority.

## 6

**攻权:** 战不必胜，不可以言战；攻不必拔，不可以言攻。不然虽刑赏不足信也。信在期前，事在未兆，故众已聚不虚散，兵出不徒归，求敌若求亡子，击敌若救溺人。

If victory is not assured, one cannot speak of engaging in battle; If a fortress is not certain to be captured, one cannot speak of launching an attack. Otherwise, even punishments and rewards will not be trusted. Trust is established before the deadline, and matters are handled before signs appear. Therefore, when troops gather, they do not disperse in vain; when soldiers march out, they do not return empty-handed. Seeking the enemy is like seeking a lost child, and striking the enemy is like rescuing someone drowning.

## 7

**攻权:** 分险者无战心，挑战者无全气，斗战者无胜兵。凡挟义而战者，贵从我起，争私结怨，应不得已。怨结虽起，待之贵后，故争必当待之，息必当备之。

Those who divide dangers have no fighting spirit; those who provoke battles have no complete morale; those who engage in reckless combat have no victorious army. Generally, those who fight under the banner of righteousness value following my lead; disputes over personal interests and grudges should only be addressed when absolutely necessary. Even if grievances arise, it is better to wait for the enemy to act first; therefore, in disputes one must await the right moment, and when tensions subside, one must prepare accordingly.

## 8

**攻权:** 兵有胜于朝廷，有胜于原野，有胜于市井，斗则得，服则失，幸以不败，此不意彼惊惧而曲胜之也。曲胜，言非全也。非全胜者，无权名。故明主战攻日，合鼓合角，节以兵刃，不求胜而胜也。

There are victories achieved in the court, on the battlefield, and even in the marketplace. In combat, one gains; in submission, one loses. If by fortune one is not defeated, this is a crooked victory gained when the enemy is unexpectedly frightened. A crooked victory is not a complete one. Those who cannot achieve complete victory have no claim to strategic authority. Therefore, when an enlightened sovereign goes to war, he unifies the drums and horns, and controls the timing and pace of weapons; without seeking victory, he nevertheless achieves it.

## 9

**攻权:** 兵有去备彻威而胜者，以其有敌矣。夫以有敌者，前死而后生。故敌一则明，明则人自战。故兵教者，其野之民皆战。今以故为之，则存亡之机也。

There are cases where an army sets aside its preparations and lays down its authority, yet still wins—this is because it has a worthy enemy. When there is a worthy enemy, those who advance forward to die will survive. Therefore, when the enemy is unified, one's own side becomes clear-sighted; when clear-sighted, every man fights for himself. Thus, through military training, even the common people in the countryside can all fight. If we do this deliberately, then it becomes the hinge of survival or destruction.

## 📖 银雀山汉简异文

1972年山东临沂银雀山一号汉墓出土的竹简中有〈攻权〉篇残简，篇末题"兵劝（权）"，与今本《攻权》对应。汉简首句作"兵以静固，以专胜"，与今本"兵以静胜，国以专胜"互有异同：

**银雀山汉简〈攻权〉释文：** 〔兵以静〕固，以槫（专）胜。力分者弱，心疑者北。□□□故进退不稿（豪），从（纵）适（敌）不禽，将吏士卒，童（动）静如身。心疑必北，是故□……无尝试，发童（动）必蚤（早），亩凌而兵，无与战矣。〔夫将帅〕，心也；群下，支（肢）节也。其心童（动）……心童（动）疑，支（肢）节……下不节童（动），唯（虽）胜为幸，不壹不……〔□□者〕败，威立者胜。凡将死其道者……□□□威在志位，志位不代（忒），威乃……□爱者，将之成者也，是故兵不□□……以名信，信在孱兆。是故众聚不虚散，兵出不徒〔归〕，□□□□亡人，击适（敌）若卜（赴）溺者。囚险者毋战心，搕战毋胜兵，佻战毋全气。凡侠（挟）议（义）□□□□□起；争私结怨，贵以不得已。□□□起□适（敌）贵先。故事必当时，□必当□，〔故兵胜〕于朝廷，胜于丧纪，胜于土功，胜于市〔井〕……□败，曲胜者，其胜全，虽不曲胜，胜劝（权）……□□□以明吾胜也。．兵劝（权）

*An army is made firm by stillness and wins by concentration of force. Where strength is divided, it is weak; where minds are doubtful, it is defeated. … Hence advance and retreat are never rash, and one does not let the enemy go by indulging them; generals, officers and soldiers move and rest as one body. When minds are doubtful, defeat is certain; therefore one never makes trial attacks, and when action is taken it must be early; … there is nothing to fight against such an army. The general is the heart, and the host below is the limbs and joints. When the heart moves … when the heart moves in doubt, the limbs and joints … if the subordinates do not move in due measure, even victory is a matter of luck; without singleness there is no … Those who are doubtful are defeated; those whose authority is established are victorious. … Authority resides in singleness of purpose; when purpose does not err, authority endures … One who is generous is the completion of a general, and therefore the army is never … trust is secured by reputation, and reputation lies in subtle omens. Hence when the multitude is gathered it does not scatter in vain, and when the army goes forth it does not return empty-handed; … seeking the enemy is like seeking a lost son, and striking the enemy is like plunging after a drowning man. Those hemmed in by danger have no will to fight, those who fight rashly have no army that wins, those who provoke battle have no full morale. Generally, when one fights upholding righteousness, …; disputes over private gain and settled grudges are best entered upon only when unavoidable. … when battle is joined, it is best to strike first. Therefore affairs must accord with the time, and measures must accord with what is fitting; hence the army is victorious in the court, victorious over mourning rites, victorious over public works, victorious over the marketplace. … one who wins by a crooked route—his victory is complete only in part; even without a crooked victory, victory by stratagem …*

> 对照要点：今本首句作"兵以静胜，国以专胜"，汉简作"兵以静固，以槫（专）胜"，"静固/静胜"用字有异，而"专"（兵力集中）一义两本皆有；汉简独有"曲胜者，其胜全，虽不曲胜，胜劝（权）"，与今本第8段"曲胜，言非全也。非全胜者，无权名"相通而文字不同——汉简强调权谋之胜非全胜，今本仅存功能性表述，思想重心发生偏移；汉简"囚险者毋战心，搕战毋胜兵，佻战毋全气"对应今本第7段"分险者无战心，挑战者无全气，斗战者无胜兵"，句序与用字互异；汉简"众聚不虚散，兵出不徒〔归〕"对应今本第6段"众已聚不虚散，兵出不徒归"，亦多出"求敌若求亡子"之意。
