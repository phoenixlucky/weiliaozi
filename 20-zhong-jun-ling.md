# 踵军令 · Command of the Follow-up Army

## 终极整合版 · Ultimate Reconstructed Text

### 1

**踵军令:** 所谓踵军者，去大军百里，期于会地，为三日熟食，前军而行，为战合之表。合表，乃起踵军，飨士，使为之战势，是谓趋战者也。

The so-called *zhong jun* refers to a force stationed one hundred li ahead of the main army, expecting to meet at a designated location. It prepares three days' worth of cooked food in advance and marches ahead of the vanguard as an indicator for engaging in battle. When the signal to engage is given, the *zhong jun* departs first, entertains and inspires the soldiers, and prepares them for battle. This is called *qu zhan*, or advancing toward combat.

### 2

**踵军令:** 兴军者，前踵军而行，合表乃起，去大军一倍其道，去踵军百里，期于会地，为六日熟食，使为战备，分卒据要害。战利则追北，按兵而趋之。踵军遇有还者诛之。所谓诸将之兵，在四奇之内者胜也。

The *xing jun* is a force that marches ahead of the *zhong jun*. When the signal to engage is given, it departs and travels twice as far from the main army as the distance between the main army and the *zhong jun*; specifically, it is one hundred li away from the *zhong jun*, expecting to meet at the designated location. It prepares six days' worth of cooked food, ensures readiness for battle, and stations detachments to occupy key strategic points. If victory is achieved in battle, pursue the defeated enemy while keeping troops under control and advancing steadily. Any member of the *zhong jun* who encounters a retreating force and fails to act will be executed. The forces commanded by various generals that fall within the four strategic formations are victorious.

### 3

**踵军令:** 兵有什伍，有分有合，豫为之职，守要塞关梁而分居之。战合表起，即皆会也。大军为计日之食起，战具无不及也，令行而起，不如令者有诛。

Troops are organized into units of ten and five, with divisions and combinations arranged in advance according to their duties. They occupy key passes and strategic locations while being stationed separately. When the signal for battle is raised, all forces immediately assemble. The main army prepares provisions based on a calculated number of days, ensuring that no battle equipment is lacking. Orders are issued and carried out accordingly; those who fail to obey the commands will be punished.

### 4

**踵军令:** 凡称分塞者，四境之内，当兴军踵军既行，则四境之民，无得行者。奉王之军命，授持符节，名为顺职之吏，非顺职之吏而行者诛之。战合表起，顺职之吏，乃行用以相参，故欲战先安内也。

Whenever a *fen sai* (division of strategic positions) is declared, within the four borders of the territory, once the *xing jun* and *zhong jun* have departed, no civilians within these borders are allowed to travel. Those who carry out the military orders of the king and hold official seals or credentials are known as officials fulfilling their proper duties. Anyone traveling without being such an authorized official will be executed. When the signal for battle is given, only officials fulfilling their proper duties are allowed to move about and coordinate with one another. Therefore, before engaging in war, it is essential first to ensure internal stability.
