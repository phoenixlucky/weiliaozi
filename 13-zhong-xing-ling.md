# 重刑令 · Severe Punishment Decree

> **终极整合版**：本章仅存武经七书本一种版本，无其他版本异文，整合版即武经七书本全文。
>
> *Ultimate reconstructed text: this chapter survives only in the Wujing Qishu recension, with no variant readings preserved in any other witness; the reconstructed text below is therefore the full text of that recension.*

## 终极整合版 · Ultimate Reconstructed Text

### 1

**重刑令:** 夫将自千人以上，有战而北，守而降，离地逃众，命曰'国贼'。身戮家残，去其籍，发其坟墓，暴其骨于市，男女公于官。自百人以上，有战而北，守而降，离地逃众，命曰'军贼'。身死家残，男女公于官。使民内畏重刑，则外轻敌。

A general commanding more than a thousand men who flees in battle, surrenders while defending, abandons his post and escapes from the ranks is called a \"guozei\" (national traitor). He shall be put to death, his family destroyed; his household registration shall be removed, his tombs exhumed, and his bones displayed in the marketplace. His sons and daughters shall be handed over to government officials for public disposition. A commander in charge of more than a hundred men who flees in battle, capitulates while defending, abandons the post and escapes from his unit is called a \"junzei\" (military bandit). He shall be killed and his family ruined; his sons and daughters shall be turned over to government officials for public disposal. If the people are made to fear severe punishments internally, they will regard their enemies lightly in external affairs.

### 2

**重刑令:** 故先王明制度于前，重威刑于后。刑重则内畏，内畏则外轻矣。

Therefore, previous sage kings established clear regulations beforehand and emphasized strict punishment afterward. Severe punishments cause internal fear; internal fear leads to a dismissive attitude toward external adversaries.
