# 分塞令 · Order on Divided Blockades

> **终极整合版**：本章仅存武经七书本一种版本，别无其他版本异文可资参校，整合版即武经七书本全文。
>
> *Ultimate reconstructed text: this chapter survives only in the Wujing Qishu recension, with no variant readings from any other witness; the reconstructed text is therefore the full Wujing Qishu text.*

## 终极整合版 · Ultimate Reconstructed Text

### 1

**分塞令:** 中军、左、右、前、后军，皆有分地，方之以行垣，而无通其交往。将有分地，帅有分地，伯有分地，皆营其沟域，而明其塞令，使非百人无得通。非其百人而入者伯诛之，伯不诛与之同罪。

The central army, the left, right, front, and rear armies all have their assigned territories. They are separated by walls like a square formation, without allowing communication or interaction between them. Generals have their assigned territories, commanders have their assigned territories, and *bo* officials also have their assigned territories. Each establishes ditches and walls around their encampments, clearly issuing orders to block passage so that no group of a hundred people or more may pass through. Anyone entering without being part of a group of at least one hundred people will be executed by the *bo* official; if the *bo* does not carry out the execution, he shall bear the same punishment.

### 2

**分塞令:** 军中纵横之道，百有二十步而立一府柱。量人与地，柱道相望，禁行清道，非将吏之符节，不得通行。采薪刍牧者皆成伍，不成伍者不得通行。吏属无节，士无伍者，横门诛之。逾分干地者，诛之。故内无干令犯禁，则外无不获之奸。

In the military encampment, along both longitudinal and transverse roads, a pillar is erected every 120 *bu*. By measuring the people and the land, these pillars are placed in sight of one another to block passage and clear roads. No one may pass without the official credentials or tokens of a general or officer. Those gathering firewood, fodder, or grazing livestock must form groups of five; those who do not form such groups may not pass through. Officials without credentials and soldiers not in groups of five shall be executed at the cross-gate. Those who overstep their assigned boundaries or encroach upon another's territory shall be executed. Therefore, if there are no violations of orders or prohibitions within, then outside there will be no evildoers who escape capture.
