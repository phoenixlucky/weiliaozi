# 勒卒令 · Command on Troop Discipline

## 1

**勒卒令:** 金、鼓、铃、旗四者各有法。鼓之则进，重鼓则击。金之则止，重金则退。铃，传令也。旗麾之左则左，麾之右则右，奇兵则反是。

The instruments of *jin* (gong), *gu* (drum), *ling* (bell-rattle), and *qi* (flag) each have their own prescribed methods. When the drum is beaten, troops advance; when struck heavily, it signals an attack. When the *jin* is sounded, troops halt; when sounded heavily, it signifies a retreat. The *ling* is used for conveying orders. When the *qi* is waved to the left, troops move left; when waved to the right, they move right. For special forces, the opposite applies.

## 2

**勒卒令:** 一鼓一击而左，一鼓一击而右。一步一鼓，步鼓也。十步一鼓，趋鼓也，音不绝，鹜鼓也。商，将鼓也。角，帅鼓也，小鼓，伯鼓也。三鼓同，则将、帅、伯其心一也。奇兵则反是。

One drumbeat and one strike to the left; one drumbeat and one strike to the right. One step per drumbeat is called *bu gu* (step drum). Ten steps to one drumbeat is *qu gu* (hurried drum); when the sound does not cease, it becomes *wu gu* (continuous drum). *Shang* refers to *jiang gu* (the general's drum). *Jue* is *shuai gu* (the commander's drum); the small drum is *bo gu* (the officer's drum). When three drums sound together, it means the general, commander, and officer are united in purpose. For special troops, the opposite applies.

## 3

**勒卒令:** 鼓失次者有诛，喧哗者有诛，不听金、鼓、铃、旗者有诛。

Those who disrupt the drum sequence shall be punished; those who create noise and commotion shall be punished; and those who disobey the signals of *jin*, *gu*, *ling* or *qi* shall also be punished.

## 4

**勒卒令:** 百人而教战，教成，合之千人。千人教成，合之万人。万人教成，合之三军。三军之众，有分有合，为大战之法，教成，试之以阅。

Train one hundred men in battle tactics; once trained successfully, combine them into a unit of one thousand. When the thousand are well-trained, unite them into ten thousand. Once ten thousand are trained, combine them to form the three armies. The forces of the three armies should be organized with both divisions and unifications, forming the method for major battles. Once trained, they are tested through inspections.

## 5

**勒卒令:** 方亦胜，圆亦胜，错斜亦胜，临险亦胜。敌在山缘而从之，敌在渊没而从之，求敌如求亡子，从之无疑，故能败敌而制其命。

They can win in square formation, in circular formation, in irregular or diagonal formations, and even when facing perilous terrain. If the enemy is on a mountain ridge, follow them; if they are in deep ravines, pursue them. Seek out the enemy as one would search for a lost child, following without hesitation — thus you can defeat the enemy and control their fate.

## 6

**勒卒令:** 夫蚤决先敌，若计不先定，虑不蚤决，则进退不定，疑生必败。故正兵贵先，奇兵贵后，或先或后，制敌者也。世将不知法者，专命而行，先击而勇，无不败者也。

To decide early and act before the enemy is essential. If plans are not decided in advance, or if deliberation does not occur promptly, then hesitation will arise over advancing or retreating, doubt will emerge, and defeat will follow. Therefore, the main force should act first; the special forces should act later. Whether acting before or after depends on the strategy to control the enemy. Generals of the world who do not understand military law act solely by personal command, strike first out of bravery alone — and none fail to be defeated.

## 7

**勒卒令:** 其举有疑而不疑，其往有信而不信，其致有迟疾而不迟疾，是三者战之累也。

When actions are uncertain yet treated as certain, when movements are intended to be trustworthy but not actually so, and when the pace of deployment is neither slow nor fast as planned — these three factors are burdens in battle.
