# 将令 · General's Commands

> **终极整合版**：以武经七书本为底本。本章仅存武经七书本一种版本，无其他版本异文可资参校，整合版即武经七书本全文。
>
> *Ultimate reconstructed text: based on the Wujing Qishu recension. This chapter survives in only the Wujing Qishu recension, with no variant readings from other versions to collate against; the reconstructed text is therefore the complete text of that recension.*

## 终极整合版 · Ultimate Reconstructed Text

### 1

**将令:** 将军受命，君必先谋于庙，行令于廷，君身以斧钺授将曰：『左、右、中军皆有分职，若逾分而上请者死，军无二令，二令者诛。留令者诛。失令者诛。』

The general receiving his commission must first deliberate in the ancestral temple, and issue orders in court. The sovereign himself presents axes and halberds to the general and says: \"The left, right, and central armies each have their assigned duties; anyone who exceeds their authority and makes requests shall be put to death. There can be no two commands in an army; those who issue dual commands shall be executed. Those who retain orders shall be executed. Those who lose the command shall be executed.\"

### 2

**将令:** 将军告曰：『出国门之外，期日中设营，表置辕门，期之，如过时则坐法。』

The general declares: \"Upon leaving the national gate, set up camp by noon. Erect standards at the camp gate, and adhere to this schedule; those who are late shall be punished according to law.\"

### 3

**将令:** 将军入营，即闭门清道，有敢行者诛，有敢高言者诛，有敢不从令者诛。

When the general enters the camp, he immediately closes the gates and clears the roads. Anyone who dares to walk there shall be executed; anyone who dares to speak loudly shall be executed; and anyone who dares not to obey orders shall be executed.
