# 束伍令 · Command on Troop Binding

## 1

**束伍令:** 束伍之令曰：五人为伍，共一符，收于将吏之所，亡伍而得伍当之。得伍而不亡有赏，亡伍不得伍，身死家残。亡长得长当之，得长不亡有赏，亡长不得长，身死家残，复战得首长，除之。亡将得将当之，得将不亡有赏，亡将不得将，坐离地遁逃之法。

The decree of Shu Wu said: Five people form a *wu*, sharing one token, collected at the place of the generals and officials; if someone leaves his *wu* and joins another, he shall be held responsible for it. Joining a new *wu* without leaving one's original *wu* will result in a reward; if one leaves his original *wu* and cannot join another, he himself shall die and his family suffer. Leaving one's leader and joining another will result in being held responsible; if one joins a new leader without leaving the original, there shall be a reward. If one leaves his original leader and cannot join another, he himself must die and his family suffer; recapturing or killing an enemy leader who has defected will exempt him from punishment. Leaving one's commander and joining another shall result in being held responsible; joining a new commander without leaving the original will bring a reward. If one abandons his original commander and cannot join another, punishment according to the law for abandoning post and fleeing shall be imposed.

## 2

**束伍令:** 战诛之法曰：什长得诛十人，伯长得诛什长，千人之将得诛百人之长，万人之将得诛千人之将，左右将军得诛万人之将，大将军无不得诛。

The law of execution in battle states: A leader of ten may execute ten men; a leader of one hundred may execute the leader of ten; a general commanding a thousand may execute leaders of a hundred; a general commanding ten thousand may execute generals commanding a thousand; left and right marshals may execute generals commanding ten thousand; and the supreme general-in-chief may execute anyone without restriction.
