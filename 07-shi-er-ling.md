# 十二陵 · Twelve Attributes

## 终极整合版 · Ultimate Reconstructed Text

### 1

**十二陵:** 威在于不变；惠在于因时；机在于应事；战在于治气；攻在于意表；守在于外饰；无过在于度数；无困在于豫备；谨在于畏小；智在于治大；除害在于果断；得众在于下人。

Power lies in remaining unchanged. Benevolence lies in adapting to the times. Opportunity lies in responding to affairs. Battle lies in regulating one's spirit and morale. Attack lies in striking beyond the opponent's expectations. Defense lies in outward appearances and displays. Avoiding excess lies in proper measure and proportion. Avoiding distress lies in prior preparation. Cautiousness lies in fearing small matters. Wisdom lies in managing great affairs. Eliminating harm lies in decisiveness. Gaining the people's support lies in humility and putting others first.

### 2

**十二陵:** 悔在于任疑；孽在于屠戮；偏在于多私；不详在于恶闻己过；不度在于竭民财；不明在于受间；不实在于轻发；固陋在于离质；祸在于好利；害在于亲小人；亡在于无所守；危在于无号令。

Regret lies in entrusting matters to those one doubts. Disaster lies in slaughter and killing. Bias lies in excessive self-interest. Misfortune lies in disliking to hear of one's own faults. Immeasurable excess lies in draining the people's wealth. Lack of clarity lies in accepting false information or deception. Unreliability lies in acting hastily and without careful consideration. Narrow-mindedness lies in separating from the essential nature or truth. Disaster lies in loving profit and personal gain. Harm lies in being close to petty or unworthy people. Perishing lies in having nothing to defend or uphold. Danger lies in the absence of commands and orders.
