# 守权 · Defensive Strategy

> **终极整合版**：以武经七书本为底本，参校银雀山汉简〈守权〉残简。凡异文择善而从，汉简独有文句补入并以校勘记标注；各版本全文列于下方版本对照。
>
> *Ultimate reconstructed text: based on the Wujing Qishu recension, collated against the Yinqueshan Han tomb bamboo-slip fragments of the "Shouquan" chapter. The best reading is adopted wherever versions differ; passages unique to the bamboo slips are restored and flagged in the collation notes. The full texts of each version follow below under Version Comparison.*

## 终极整合版 · Ultimate Reconstructed Text

### 1

**守权:** 凡守者，进不郭围，退不亭障，以御战，非善者也。豪杰雄俊，坚甲利兵，劲弩强矢，尽在郭中。乃收窖廪，毁拆而入保，令客气十百倍，而主之气不半焉，敌攻者伤之甚也。

Generally, in defense, if one does not advance to the outer walls for encirclement nor retreat to strongholds and barriers, yet still tries to engage in combat, it is not good strategy. The heroes, the brave, strong armor, sharp weapons, powerful crossbows, and sturdy arrows are all concentrated within the city walls. Then they collect the stored grain from pits and granaries, demolish outer structures, and retreat into the protected area, causing the enemy's morale to increase ten or a hundred times while the defenders' morale is not even half that. The attackers will inflict severe casualties.

> 校勘记 · Collation note：①"劲弩强矢"：汉简作"〔劲弩〕仁（韧）矢"，"仁"为"韧"之通假，义同，从今本；②"毁拆"：汉简作"毁折"，义同，从今本；③"令客气十百倍，而主之气不半焉"：汉简作"令客气数什百倍，而主人气不半□□□者"，文义相合；④"敌攻者伤之甚也"：汉简作"伤守甚者也"，从今本。

### 2

**守权:** 然而世将弗能知。

Yet generals of the world do not understand this.

> 校勘记 · Collation note：①"然而世将弗能知"：汉简残存"然而世□……"，与今本互证，从今本。

### 3

**守权:** 夫守者，不失其险者也。守法：城一丈，十人守之，工食不与焉。出者不战，言者不战，商者不战，三者不战，则城可守也。

Defense means not losing one's strategic advantages. The rule of defense: for every zhang of city wall, ten men guard it; laborers and cooks are not counted among them. Those who go out do not fight, those who speak do not fight, those who trade do not fight; if these three types do not fight, then the city can be defended.

> 校勘记 · Collation note：①"守法：城一丈，十人守之"：汉简作"守法：丈，□人守"，可证"城一丈十人守"之制古已有之，从今本。

### 4

**守权:** 故守城者，必先计其食物，因其材，因其民，出者不战，守者不出。一当十，十当百，百当千，千当万。然后可以备战，城可守也。

Therefore, those who defend a city must first calculate their food supplies, make use of available materials, and utilize their population. Those who go out do not fight, and those who defend do not leave their posts. One man should equal ten, ten equal a hundred, a hundred equal a thousand, and a thousand equal ten thousand. Only then can one prepare for battle and the city be defended.

> 校勘记 · Collation note：①"出者不战，守者不出"：汉简作"而守者不出，出者不守"，文义相合；②"一当十，十当百，百当千，千当万"：汉简作"一而当十，十而当百，百而当千万"，表述有异，从今本。

### 5

**守权:** 城坚而救诚，则愚智老少不惧，尽其力以守其城。守一年则救至，守一年则救者利，救至则守者利矣。其有必救之军，则有必战之民；无必救之军，则无必战之民。城坚而救不诚，则愚智老少皆惧，此人情也。开其廪庾，救其诚，虽不能止，必鼓其豪杰雄俊，坚甲利兵，劲弩强矢并于前，么么毁瘠者并于后。

If the city is strong and rescue is earnestly needed, then even foolish men and women will not fail to protect it, offering all their resources and blood for its defense. A city that can hold out for a year allows the defenders to have an advantage over attackers, while those who come to rescue will also have an advantage over the defenders. If there is an army certain to come to its rescue, then there will be a people certain to fight; if there is no army certain to come to its rescue, then there will be no people certain to fight. If the city is strong but rescue efforts are not sincere, then even foolish men and foolish women will weep at the battlements—this is human nature. If they open their granaries to provide relief in such a situation, it still cannot prevent disaster. They must rally their outstanding heroes and valiant men, concentrating strong armor, sharp weapons, and powerful crossbows with sturdy arrows at the front, while placing weak and emaciated soldiers in the rear.

> 校勘记 · Collation note：①"其有必救之军，则有必战之民；无必救之军，则无必战之民"：汉简独有文句（释文作"其有必救之军，则有必〔战之民；无〕必救之〔军，则无必战之民〕"），今本武经七书本无，强调救兵与守民相互依存，补入；②"么么毁瘠者"：汉简作"则幼□毁□并于后"，从今本；③"城坚而厚，士民众篡（选），薪食经□，〔弩〕劲矢仁（韧），矛戟□□，□□策也"：汉简以排比详述守城之备，今本以"必先计其食物，因其材，因其民"及"城坚"概括之，互有详略，从今本。

### 6

**守权:** 十万之军顿于城下，救必开之，守必出之。出据要塞，但救其后，无绝其粮道，中外相应。

An army of a hundred thousand halts before the city walls; rescue forces will certainly open the gates, and defenders will inevitably sally out. They emerge to occupy key strongholds, merely rescuing from behind, without cutting off their supply routes—thus achieving coordination between the interior and exterior forces.

> 校勘记 · Collation note：①"十万之军"：汉简作"五万之□□"（"五万之□□诚必救"），从今本；②"出据要塞，但救其后，无绝其粮道"：汉简作"关之其后，出要塞，担击其后，毋通其量（粮）食"，两本详略互异，且"毋通其量（粮）食"与今本"无绝其粮道"义反，从今本；③两本并有"中外相应"，协同防御之义相承。

### 7

**守权:** 此救而示之不诚，示之不诚，则倒敌而待之者也。后其壮，前其老，彼敌无前，守不得而止矣，此守权之谓也。

This is a form of rescue that appears insincere; by appearing insincere, one turns the enemy around and waits for them. Place the strong in the rear and the elderly at the front; if the enemy dares not advance, then the defenders cannot be stopped—this is what is meant by defensive strategy.

### 银雀山汉简

1972年山东临沂银雀山一号汉墓出土的竹简中有〈守权〉篇残简，存守城方略之论，与今本《守权》互有详略：

**银雀山汉简〈守权〉释文：** ……〔劲弩〕仁（韧）矢尽于郭中……毁折入此，令客气数什百倍，而主人气不半□□□者，伤守甚者也。然而世□……而守者不出，出者不守。守法：丈，□人守，□……一而当十，十而当百，百而当千万。……城坚而厚，士民众篡（选），薪食经□，〔弩〕劲矢仁（韧），矛戟□□，□□策也。攻者□□□□万之众乃称。其有必救之军，则有必〔战之民；无〕必救之〔军，则无必战之民〕……遇（愚）夫童妇无不敝城尽资……则固不尚。鼓其[蒿/木]豪桀（杰）俊雄，坚甲利兵，劲弩仁（韧）矢并于前，则幼□毁□并于后，五万之□□诚必救，关之其后，出要塞，担击其后，毋通其量（粮）食，中外相应……

*When the strong crossbows and tough arrows within the outer city are exhausted … when the attackers' spirit has multiplied tenfold and a hundredfold while the defenders' spirit has not reached half, that is the gravest injury to defense. … he who defends does not go forth, and he who goes forth does not defend. The rule of defense: for every zhang of wall, so many men defend it … one man counts as ten, ten as a hundred, a hundred as ten thousand. … When the walls are strong and thick, the officers and men are well chosen, firewood and food are in due supply, the crossbows are strong and the arrows tough, and spears and halberds … such is the plan. The attacker can only be called a full force when it numbers ten thousand. If there is an army certain to come to its rescue, then there will be a people certain to fight; if there is no army certain to come to its rescue, then there will be no people certain to fight. … even the foolish man and the young woman exhaust the city and its resources … then defense is not to be prized. Rouse the heroic and the valiant, array stout armor and sharp weapons, strong crossbows and tough arrows in front, while the young and the broken follow behind; when fifty thousand men are truly resolved to rescue, close in on their rear, issue from the fortresses, strike the enemy from behind, cut off the passage of their grain, and respond from within and without …*

> 对照要点：今本第1段作"劲弩强矢，尽在郭中"，汉简作"〔劲弩〕仁（韧）矢尽于郭中"，"仁"为"韧"之通假；今本第3段作"守法：城一丈，十人守之"，汉简作"守法：丈，□人守"，可证"城一丈十人守"之制古已有之；今本第4段作"一当十，十当百，百当千，千当万"，汉简作"一而当十，十而当百，百而当千万"，表述有异；汉简独有"其有必救之军，则有必战之民；无必救之军，则无必战之民"，强调救兵与守民相互依存，及"关之其后，出要塞，担击其后，毋通其量（粮）食，中外相应"的协同防御——今本第6段"中外相应"与之相承而详略互异。
