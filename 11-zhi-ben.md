# 治本 · Fundamental Governance

## 终极整合版 · Ultimate Reconstructed Text

### 1

**治本:** 凡治人者何？曰：'非五谷无以充腹，非丝麻无以盖形。'故充腹有粒，盖形有缕，夫在芸耨，妻在机杼，民无二事，则有储蓄，夫无雕文刻镂之事，女无绣饰纂组之作。

What is governing people? It is said: \"Without the five grains, there would be no way to fill one's belly; without silk and hemp, there would be no means to cover one's body.\" Therefore, to fill the belly requires grains, and to cover the body requires threads; men are engaged in farming, while women work at the loom. With no other pursuits for the people, there will be accumulation of resources. Men do not engage in carving or engraving, and women do not partake in embroidery or elaborate decorative works.

### 2

**治本:** 木器液，金器腥，圣人饮于土，食于土，故埏埴以为器，天下无费。今也，金木之性不寒，而衣绣饰；马牛之性食草饮水，而给菽粟。是治失其本，而宜设之制也。

Wooden vessels become damp, and metal ones emit a metallic smell; thus, sage people drink from earthenware and eat with earthen utensils. Therefore, they mold clay to make vessels, resulting in no waste throughout the world. Nowadays, although metal and wood do not provide warmth, people still wear embroidered decorations; Horses and oxen naturally eat grass and drink water, yet they are fed beans and grains. This is a failure to grasp the fundamental principles of governance, and it represents an inappropriate system established.

### 3

**治本:** 春夏夫出于南亩，秋冬女练于布帛，则民不困。今短褐不蔽形，糟糠不充腹，失其治也。

If in spring and summer men work in the fields, and in autumn and winter women spin and weave cloth, then the people will not be burdened. Now, coarse clothes do not cover one's body, and gruel and chaff do not fill one's belly—this is a failure in governance.

### 4

**治本:** 古者土无肥瘠，人无勤惰，古人何得，今人何失耶？耕者不终亩，织者日断机，而奈何饥寒。盖古治之行，今治之止也。

In ancient times, the land had no distinction between fertile and barren, nor did people differ in diligence or laziness; what did the ancients gain that we have lost today? Those who till do not finish their fields, and those who weave break their looms daily—how then can hunger and cold be avoided? This is because the governance of ancient times was practiced, while that of today has ceased.

### 5

**治本:** 夫谓治者，使民无私也。民无私，则天下为一家，无私耕私织，共寒其寒，共饥其饥。故如有子十人，不加一饭；有子一人，不损一饭，焉有喧呼酖酒以败善类乎？

What is meant by good governance is to ensure that the people have no private interests. When the people have no private desires, then the world becomes one family; there is neither private farming nor private weaving, and all share in each other's cold and hunger. Therefore, even if someone has ten children, no extra meal is added; if one has only one child, no meal is reduced—how then could there be shouting and drunken revelry that harms the virtuous?

### 6

**治本:** 民相轻佻，则欲心与争夺之患起矣。横生于一夫，则民私饭有储食，私用有储财，民一犯禁，而拘以刑治，乌有以为人上也。善政执其制，使民无私，则为下不敢私，则无为非者矣。

When people treat each other with frivolity, desires arise along with conflicts and struggles. When such behavior arises among one person, the people begin to hoard food for private use and accumulate wealth for personal purposes. When the people violate prohibitions even once, they are restrained by punishment—how can this be considered ruling over others? Good governance upholds the system to ensure that people have no private interests; thus, those beneath will dare not act privately and there will be none who commit wrongdoing.

### 7

**治本:** 反本缘理，出乎一道，则欲心去，争夺止，囹圄空，野充粟多，安民怀远，外无天下之难，内无暴乱之事，治之至也。

Returning to the root and following principles, emerging from a single path—then desires vanish, contention ceases, prisons become empty, fields overflow with grain, people are at peace and distant lands are won over. There will be no troubles in the world outside, nor internal disorders within; this is the highest level of governance.

### 8

**治本:** 苍苍之天，莫知其极，帝王之君，谁为法则？往世不可及，来世不可待，求己者也。

The vast blue sky knows no limit—who can know its end? The emperors and kings—whose example should they follow as a model? The past cannot be reached, the future cannot be awaited—it is up to oneself to seek.

### 9

**治本:** 所谓天子者四焉：一曰神明；二曰垂光；三曰洪叙；四曰无敌。此天子之事也。

What is meant by an emperor of heaven includes four aspects: first, being divine and enlightened; second, radiating brilliance; third, great order; fourth, being invincible. These are the matters of an emperor of heaven.

### 10

**治本:** 野物不为牺牲，杂学不为通儒。今说者曰：'百里之海，不能饮一夫；三尺之泉，足止三军渴。'臣谓：'欲生于无度，邪生于无禁。'

Wild creatures do not become sacrifices, and miscellaneous learning does not make one a learned Confucian scholar. Now some say: \"A sea within a hundred li cannot quench the thirst of one man; a spring three chi deep is enough to quench the thirst of an entire army.\" I say: \"Desires arise from lack of limits, and evil arises from absence of prohibitions.\"

### 11

**治本:** 太上神化，其次因物，其下在于无夺民时，无损民财。夫禁必以武而成，赏必以文而成。

The highest level is to transform through divine influence; the next is to follow nature's course. The lowest standard lies in not seizing the people's time and not depleting their wealth. Prohibitions must be enforced through military strength to succeed, while rewards must be implemented with civility to achieve effectiveness.
