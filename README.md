# 尉缭子 — Wei Liaozi

> **还原最原始的《尉缭子》，中文原文 + 英文翻译对照版**
>
> **Restoring the most authentic Wei Liaozi — a bilingual edition with the Chinese original and English translation side by side.**

《尉缭子》是中国古代著名兵书，战国时期（公元前475年—公元前221年）尉缭所著。它融合了法家、兵家思想，既讲军事战略战术，也论政治经济制度，是中国古典军事理论的重要著作。原有24篇，本书收录全本。

*The Wei Liaozi is a celebrated Chinese military classic attributed to Wei Liao of the Warring States period (475–221 BCE). It synthesizes Legalist and Military-school thought, addressing both military strategy and tactics as well as political and economic institutions. Originally comprising 24 chapters, this collection presents the complete received text.*

## 项目特色 · Features

### 版本对照 · Version Comparison

本项目汇集了《尉缭子》的四种重要版本/文本来源：

*This project brings together four important versions/textual sources of the Wei Liaozi:*

| 版本 Version | 时代 Era | 篇数 Chapters | 特点 Features |
|------|------|------|------|
| **武经七书本**（底本）<br>Wujing Qishu edition (base) | 北宋 · N. Song | 24篇全 · all 24 | 最完整的传世本 · the most complete received text |
| **群书治要本**（异文）<br>Qunshu Zhiyao edition | 唐·魏徵编 · Tang, ed. Wei Zheng | 4篇选录 · 4 chapters | 唐代抄本，文字有重要差异 · Tang manuscript with significant textual variants |
| **太平御览引文**（佚文）<br>Taiping Yulan quotations | 宋·李昉编 · Song, ed. Li Fang | 多条 · many entries | 类书引文，保存古本面貌 · encyclopedia quotations preserving archaic readings |
| **银雀山汉简**（出土文献）<br>Yinqueshan bamboo slips | 西汉早期 · early W. Han | 6篇+佚文 · 6 chapters + fragments | 最接近古本的出土文本，见 [附录：银雀山汉简辑录](appendix-yinqueshan-bamboo-slips.md) · excavated text closest to the ancient original |

### 类书引文辑录 · Quotations from Medieval Encyclopedias

从《太平御览》《通典》《卫公兵法》《墨子闲诂》《初学记》《唐李问对》等唐宋文献中辑录了《尉缭子》引文，与传世本逐条对照，揭示文本演变轨迹。

*Quotations of the Wei Liaozi are collected from Tang-Song sources such as the Taiping Yulan, Tongdian, Wei Gong Bingfa (Li Jing's Methods), Mozi Xiangu, Chuxue Ji, and Tang Taizong Li Jing Wendui, then compared item by item with the received text to trace the evolution of the work.*

### 银雀山汉简 · The Yinqueshan Bamboo Slips

收录1972年山东临沂银雀山汉墓出土的《尉缭子》六篇汉简释文（〈兵谈〉〈攻权〉〈守权〉〈将理〉〈原官〉〈兵令〉）及零散佚文，与今本逐段对照，呈现今本在流传中删削的"胜于丧纪、土功、市井"等治国维度、"小鱼渔渊"战略层级理论与"不杀夭胎"军事伦理等佚失内容。

*Transcriptions of the six chapters recovered from the Han tomb at Yinqueshan, Linyi, Shandong (1972) — Bing Tan, Gong Quan, Shou Quan, Jiang Li, Yuan Guan, Bing Ling — together with scattered fragments, are set against the received text passage by passage, revealing what was excised in transmission: the governance dimensions of "prevailing over mourning rites, public works, and markets," the tiered strategy of "small fish fish the deep waters," and the martial-ecological ethic of "do not kill the unborn, do not cut unripe timber."*

### 文本校勘 · Textual Collation

每篇异文附有校勘记，标注各版本文字差异，包括：

*Each set of variants is accompanied by collation notes recording textual differences across versions, including:*

- 人名异文 · names：公子心 / 将军子正 / 将军子心
- 地名异文 · place names：盟津 / 棘津、济水 / 清水
- 数字异文 · numerals：二万二千五百 / 万二千
- 句式异文 · syntax：问答体 / 陈述体 (dialogue vs. statement)
